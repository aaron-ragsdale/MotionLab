The model uses timestamps and raw position sequences, resampled to 12 steps, plus position summaries and displacement. Forest votes are not calibrated probabilities. Low visibility or insufficient history suppresses predictions. A game should use a confidence gate, idle class and cooldown rather than triggering on every prediction.

Privacy: the camera and inference run on this device. The browser downloads MediaPipe runtime and model assets from public CDNs. Image coordinates are estimates, and world landmarks are inferred relative to the hips, not calibrated camera-space measurements. Hand points are wrist, pinky, index and thumb; feet have ankle, heel and foot index. This is not a 21-point hand model.
